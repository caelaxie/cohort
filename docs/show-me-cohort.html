Product definition — not the leftover app

Cohort is the app.
Hatch is the lead bot.

Open-source Grok Bot for you, on this Mac. Named teammates. Hatch makes more bots and can run the crew. They use the browser you are already logged into, and they ask before they send, post, buy, or delete.

Two names

Cohort The product. The window you open. Your Grok Bot alternative.
Hatch The lead bot inside Cohort. It hatches teammates and can coordinate them.

What you open

Home is a list of bots, not workspaces. Hatch sits at the top. You can talk to Hatch alone, or sit in a room with Hatch and the bots it made.
